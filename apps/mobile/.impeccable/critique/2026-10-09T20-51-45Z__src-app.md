## Design Specificity Verdict

The book line (faces above, note bubbles below, dashed pace tick), "YOU ARE HERE · P. 188", blurred notes ahead with "Show note", and "Where are you in The Hobbit?" belong to this product alone. The frame around them (My books cards with cover/bar/full-width button, the Clubs tab, the You settings list, the start-a-club and join forms) could be any app in paper colours; palette and Literata carry the brand, not the structure.

## Overall Impression

The heart works (one-tap logging, spoiler-safe notes). What still feels complicated is structural: a club book has two homes, starting and finishing lead nowhere, and every screen has two "main" buttons. Biggest opportunity: one page per book, with the club as a layer on it.

## What's Working

1. The book line and spoiler model: progress, people and notes on one drawing placed by position, edition-agnostic; bubbles announce "1 note at p. 250 by Nora Notes, ahead of where you are"; blurred text is hidden from screen readers.
2. The Update page sheet: one big figure, "of 320", +5/+10/+25, % for e-readers, last-page detection; several books are 3 taps each.
3. The book page as one decision: Start reading or Want to read, the picked edition explained in one line, "Different edition?" tucked away.

## Priority Issues

[P1] One book has two homes. Reading page (all 11 notes, no faces, no club link) and club page (7 club notes, faces, its own Update page and "+ Note", plus a "Your book" link back). Why: main source of "too complex"; friends' positions are 3 taps from where you log. Fix: one page per book; for a club book the reading page shows the club's faces, one club line ("Thursday Night Readers · next: Fri 16 Oct, read to p. 120") and a Club/Everyone filter; the club page becomes people, meetings, settings; My books cards for club books add "Thursday Night Readers · Sam p. 214". Command: shape, then distill.

[P1] Starting and finishing are dead ends. Start lands on a "Not started" card with nothing asking where you are; finishing makes the card vanish into Read, with no moment when every blurred note opens. Fix: after Start, open "Where are you in Piranesi?" with "Just started"; on finishing, an ending sheet ("You finished The Hobbit", "All 11 notes are open now", "Leave a last thought", "Next up" from Want to read); "I finished it" instant with Undo. Command: onboard, delight.

[P1] Competing main buttons and a button wall. Red "Update page" plus red "+ Note" on detail pages; full-width "Update page" takes ~40% of each My books card, so three books fill the phone. Fix: slim rows (~88px) with a compact "p. 188 · Update" pill, 5-6 books per screen; on detail pages the progress figure is the update control and "+ Note" the one red. Command: distill, layout.

[P2] Club setup is scattered. Book, finish date, meeting and invite take ~14 taps across 5 screens; the finish date is under gear › Change dates; owners must separately press "Start reading the book". Fix: 3 steps after "Start a club" (Book → Finish by, skippable → Invite); start the owner's reading automatically; settings split into "This book" and "Club". Command: shape, onboard.

[P2] Faces on the line are hard to read and sometimes wrong. Faces closer than 30px merge and report your position for everyone; 10px initials (detector agrees); one badge style for people and notes. Fix: never merge "you"; label groups with their range; add "Sam is 26 pages ahead · Lena is on p. 61"; distinct people badge; initials at least 11px. Command: clarify, harden.

## Persona Red Flags

Casey (one hand): ⋯, gear and invite in the top-right corner; "+ Note" covers "Members"; "188" in the Update sheet is a placeholder, so Save does nothing; the keyboard opens even to tap +10.
Jordan (first time): unlabelled "+" is the only add-book control; "Read" beside "Reading" reads as a verb; no legend; "Your book" vs the club's book; joining takes Continue then Join; a new book says "Not started".
Sam (screen reader): merged face announces the wrong position for Sam Okafor; faces can't be focused, so who's where needs the Members tab below the fold; ~33 tab stops of React/Reply/More before "You are here"; blurred spoiler text still matches find-in-page.
Parent setting up a family club: no guided order, buried finish date, easy to forget starting your own reading; joiners meet "Start reading the book", "I have another edition" and maybe a page-count question.

## Minor Observations

Desktop detail pages hide the sidebar (a lone 600px column with "Back"). "06:30 PM" leading zero. Club notes repeat "· Thursday Night Readers ·". The notes feed starts at page 1, not at "You are here". Pace p. 68 vs the meeting's "read to p. 120": show the meeting goal when there is one. The note sheet hint wraps to 3 lines. The ISBN hint is always visible. Want to read's "Find a book" leads to a page whose main button is "Start reading". Spinners, not placeholder shapes; the tab title flashes "Editions". Two note composers (NoteSheet, NoteComposer) could drift. WebChrome's 4px radius is off the scale.

## Questions to Consider

- Could the page number itself be the update control, leaving "+ Note" as the one red action?
- What if finishing were the moment the app celebrates: every note opens, your club sees "Rae finished", you leave a last thought?
- Does "You" earn a tab, or would My books · Clubs plus an avatar menu be simpler?
