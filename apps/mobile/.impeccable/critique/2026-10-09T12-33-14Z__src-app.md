## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM: warm voice (paper palette, bookcloth red, Literata, plain copy), but generic structure (stacked bordered cards with an H2 each, settings-style forms). Authored for this product only in the notes timeline (serif notes, "YOU ARE HERE · P. 142" rule, blurred notes ahead). Page numbers live in grey metadata and progress is a generic bar, so "the book is the timeline" isn't expressed.

Deterministic scan: CLI 0 findings over apps/mobile/src. In-page detector on the reading page: 3 findings, all false positives (expo-image cross-fade start frame flagged as buried raster; two "layout property animation" hits on an invisible 0x0 fixed element that the safe-area library appears to own). Problems are compositional, not mechanical.

## Overall Impression

Calm, readable, accessible, but every screen gives the most important thing the same weight as the least important. Biggest opportunity: make logging a page one step from home, and let the layout look like a book (marginalia, a bookmark), not a settings page.

## What's Working

- Notes timeline: book order + "you are here" + blur makes the spoiler promise visible and trustworthy; the signature.
- Typography and palette: Literata on warm paper with bookcloth red; contrast passes in both themes.
- Labelled progress rows: names and pages beside every bar, pace tick, "you" in the accent; no legend, no colour-only meaning.

## Priority Issues

- [P1] The core loop is buried: home → small row → reading page → type → Save; on phone the form shares the first screen with full-size "I finished it"/"Stop reading"; Save is pale pink until typing; home's biggest button is "Start a club". Fix: log control on home's current-book card; Finish/Stop into a quiet More. Commands: layout, distill.
- [P1] Card soup flattens the hierarchy: identical bordered cards with equal H2s (club 7, reading 5). Fix: drop most card chrome; typographic hierarchy with small-caps heads and hairline rules; one filled surface per screen for the interactive focus. Commands: layout, typeset.
- [P1] Owner chrome above content: club page first phone screen = Change dates / Change book or edition / Mark as finished; "Copy invite link" is a filled primary mid-page. Fix: one "Manage" entry; invite as a quiet row; first screen = book, who is where, next meeting. Command: distill.
- [P2] "The book is the timeline" isn't visible: page numbers in metadata; generic bar. Fix: notes as marginalia with a serif page gutter; "you are here" as a bookmark ribbon; progress across the book's span with meeting targets. Commands: typeset, bolder (targeted).
- [P2] Desktop wastes the width: 560px column in 1280px; club page scrolls through 7 cards. Fix: two columns at ≥1024px (book + notes main; progress, meetings, members in a side rail). Command: adapt.
